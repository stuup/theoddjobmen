    FAQ

    Common questions

    Are you insured?

    Yes, we're fully insured for both domestic and commercial work.

    Is any job too small?

    No job is too small. We're happy to do one shelf or a single door. If you have a few little jobs, put them on one list and we'll do them in one visit.

    How do I get a quote?

    The quickest way is to WhatsApp a few photos to 07709 674761. You can also call, or fill in the quote form. Quotes are always free and no-obligation.

    How does window cleaning work?

    We use either a pure-water pole system or traditional hand cleaning. On every traditional clean, we clean the glass, frames, sills and doors. Prices start at £25 for up to 6 windows.

    How do you measure gutters, fascias and soffits?

    We price by the total length in metres, starting at £45 for up to 10 metres. If you're not sure, tell us the type of house (for example a 3-bed semi) or send a photo and we'll work it out.

    Do you do electrics, plumbing and gas?

    Plumbing and electrical work on kitchen and bathroom jobs is certified. Gas work, such as fitting a gas hob, is done by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

    Can you supply the materials?

    Yes. We can supply and fit doors, windows, kitchens and bathrooms. If you've already bought your own materials, we can just fit them.

    Do you work with landlords and businesses?

    Yes. We do end-of-tenancy cleans and repairs, commercial cleans for small businesses, and general property maintenance.
